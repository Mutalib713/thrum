Calls use the first 45 seconds of a song, repeated until you answer.

21. About Thrum
About Thrum
THRUM
Hear it. Feel it.
Why Thrum

I wanted my phone to do more than play sound. I wanted to feel the music too.

I'd seen iPhones sync vibration with music, and I wanted that on Android, made from the songs already on my phone. So I built Thrum.

For callsWon't work yet
Your phone is on Silent

Android throws the vibration away before it reaches the motor. No app can get around that.

It has to be Vibrate, not Silent. Both look like "no sound", but Silent means nothing vibrates at all.

25. This phone can't do it
THRUM
This phone can't do it

Thrum needs a motor that can change strength. Yours has one speed. Every rhythm would reach you as the same flat buzz your phone already makes, and no setting, update, or future version of this app will change that.

Motor: yes. Strength control: no.

No button here, on purpose
Sketch note: once a cheap phone is tested (Task 6), this might become an honest "you'll feel the timing, not the strength". Not before.
