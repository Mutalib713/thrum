## My Haptics and Music

- [ ] My Haptics → make a haptic from a **video** while on the **Videos** filter: you stay on Videos,
      and the new "Making its haptic…" row is at the **top**.
- [ ] Delete a haptic: the row slides away, "Haptic deleted · Undo" shows; tap **Undo**: it comes back.
- [ ] Play something, then delete it while it plays: playback **stops**.
- [ ] Music → a song's **⋮** → **Copy to Thrum Originals**: it appears under Thrum Originals.
- [ ] **See all ›** opens the Originals page; it plays; **⋮ → Remove from Thrum Originals** takes it out.
